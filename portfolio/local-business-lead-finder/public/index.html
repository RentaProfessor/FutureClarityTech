Lead Finder

A small web agency's internal tool for finding local businesses whose websites are broken, parked, for sale or out of date, and for showing each owner what's wrong in a two-page PDF audit.

  • Find every business of a kind within 1 to 10 miles of a neighborhood or ZIP code, from a business list served with the site.
  • Check each website: unregistered, expired, parked and for-sale domains, placeholder pages, broken or slow sites, the site builder and booking tools it uses, and the search and accessibility basics.
  • Score each business from 0 to 100 as a lead, then save the good ones to a call list with follow-ups.
  • Audit: download a two-page PDF for the owner, written in the browser with no PDF library.

Open the demo

Everything in this demo is fictional: the businesses, their phone numbers (all 555-01xx) and their websites (all on the reserved .test domain). Website checks are canned results that the real checker produced from synthetic pages, and your call list stays in this browser. The production version is internal to FutureClarity Technologies.
